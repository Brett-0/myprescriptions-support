Common questions

How do I add a prescription?

When your pharmacy or prescriber sends you an eScript SMS, open the message, press and hold the link, choose Share, and pick Add Prescription. The app opens the prescription page, reads the medication, date and number of repeats remaining, and fills the form in for you. You can also paste the link into the app by hand, or type the details yourself.

Add Prescription isn't in my share sheet

iOS hides newly installed share options until you switch them on, so you need to do this once. Open the share sheet, swipe the row of app icons to the left until you reach the end, and tap More. Find Add Prescription in the list and turn it on. While you are there, drag it to the top of the list using the handle on the right, and it will appear in the main row from then on instead of being tucked away.

Why can't I just tap the link in the message?

Tapping an eScript link always opens it in Safari. Apple only lets an app take over a web link when the owner of that website has specifically authorised the app, and prescription links belong to the eScript service rather than to me. Sharing the link is the supported way to hand it to the app.

Why didn't my repeat count go down after I filled a script?

The app has no connection to your pharmacy and is never told when a prescription is dispensed. Each time a script is filled you receive a new SMS with a new link, and sharing that new link into the app updates the count. If the app recognises it as the next repeat of something you already have, it will offer to update that entry rather than creating a second one.

When do I get reminded?

Two reminders per prescription, both at 9am: one thirty days before it expires, and one a week before. Reminders are scheduled on your phone and arrive even if you never open the app. There are no reminders about repeat counts, because your phone has no way to watch that number in the background.

What does archiving do?

Archiving hides a prescription from the Active, Low and Expired tabs and stops its reminders, but keeps everything. It stays under the Archive tab and you can restore it at any time. Deleting is the only action that removes anything permanently.

Does my information leave my phone?

No. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. Everything you enter stays in the app's private storage on your device. See the privacy policy for the detail.

How do I delete everything?

Open Settings inside the app and choose Delete All Data. Deleting the app itself also removes all of its data. Neither can be undone, so export a backup first if you might want it later.

Can I move my data to a new phone?

Yes. Use Export Data in Settings to produce a backup, then Import Data on the other phone. Because nothing is stored in the cloud, this is a manual step.

Something not working?

If a prescription link won't read properly, the most useful thing you can send me is what the prescription page itself shows — the medication line and the number of supplies remaining — along with what the app filled in instead. That usually tells me straight away what changed.
